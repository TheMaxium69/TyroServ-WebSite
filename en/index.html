The server

What is TyroServ?

TyroServ is a modded Minecraft PVP Faction server built for competitive and community-driven gameplay. Each season brings a fresh start, new mods, and unique challenges.

PVP Faction

Form alliances, claim territories and dominate rival factions in an open-world PVP environment.

Custom Mods

We develop our own mod "the TyroMod" for a unique and original experience exclusively crafted for TyroServ.

Community

A tight-knit community gathered on Discord to organise events, wars and seasonal challenges.

Seasonal Resets

Each season starts fresh for everyone: new mod, new map, new economy, new opportunities.
